# First hour: Trying to figure things out
Before starting the project I wrote done the rough idea I head about what I was gonna make.
Then I started searching for parts...
It was quite the hustle, also because I couldn't decinde if I should use KiCad or EasyEDA, as I was more familiar with KiCad but in the guide-files they have used EasyEDA.
I decided on using KiCad.
ALSO as Aliexpress and other shipping companies from china now have a 3€ tarif per Item, it was more cost effective to search for parts in local online shops, therefore I needed to find out if I could even get the parts, which I needed for the project.
At this stage I've used AI to find out how I could make this project quite cheap. 

SO THATS WHY I DECIDED TO DITCH MICROCONTROLLER BOARDS and thus started using the UDPI manually programmed ATtiny-1614.
My first time using it and therefore designing a board for it.

# Hours 2-3: Big switcharounds
At this point I started switching around and figuring out different parts. And thats where I decided to also switch from the through-hole parts, which I (and a lot of other ppl) usually use, to SMDs.
I don't know if I'll regret this when I start soldering everything together when the time comes, but it is what it is.

# Hours 3-6: DESIGN HELL
From then on I got tired. And everything begun to feel really tedious. I wanted to make my board look really cool. But again, again and again some things went wrong...
It was also my first time trying to make my PCB look like something special.
I learned how to use images and text etc.
BUT SOME THINGS I JUST COULD NOT FIGURE OUT. 
And I think, that I would've needed more pcb layers by now.
Things like, making a copper area and adding the f-mask back on again, to make black stars with a white soldermask outline on them.
Buuuuttt it is what it is, and I am actually quite happy on how my first hackcard turned out :>
